# Suggested working schedule (adapt to event timing)

- **09:00-09:20** Company problem, scoring criteria and handoff expectations.
- **09:20-10:20** Sheets schema, historical import and identity matching.
- **10:20-11:40** Email classification, quote/order extraction and attachments.
- **11:40-12:00** Dashboard and validation checkpoint.
- **12:00-13:00** Lunch, tour and optional continuing discussion.
- **13:00-14:05** Daily triggers, outreach drafts, reliability checks.
- **14:05-14:45** New account copy/export test, fixes and demo prep.
- **15:00 onward** Presentations and judging.

Students should not spend most of the day on HTML polish. A correctly populated Google Sheets CRM is mandatory; a beautiful separate web UI is optional.
