# Outreach decision and writing prompts

## Lead prioritization

Read only approved, structured CRM data and linked evidence. Rank up to 10 customer contacts based on: unresolved quote, prior confirmed purchase, likely relevant reorder cycle, elapsed time since last response, and current open customer needs. Return customer ID, reason, evidence message IDs, suggested next action, and whether contact has opted out. Do not invent annual reorder cycles or claimed interest. Exclude suppliers, opted-out marketing contacts and uncertain identities.

## Drafting

Produce a professional concise one-to-one email that references one verified historical detail (product, event, last quote) and requests a single reasonable next action. Avoid generic spam and misleading urgency. Don't reveal supplier costs. If an unresolved customer request exists, prioritize serving that request over new promotion. Return drafts only; nothing is transmitted.

## Example (fictional)

Input: Acme Events requested a revised 3,000-unit tote quote Q-1001-R1. Artwork is still pending. No response since quote revision.

Output draft subject: "Following up on the revised tote bag quote"

Output body: "Hi Sarah, I wanted to follow up on the revised quote for 3,000 natural canvas tote bags with two-sided navy printing. We're still waiting on the final artwork, and I can help confirm the production schedule once it's available. Does the updated quantity and pricing still work for your event?"

Output evidence: `SWAG-TOTE-01` conversation + message containing `Q-1001-R1`.

This is a **sample**, not an actual queued send. Verify quote validity and timing before using in a real account.
